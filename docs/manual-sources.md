# Sources that need a login (can't be scripted)

Everything in the numbered genre folders was downloaded automatically. The
sources below are all still **free**, but each one sits behind an account, an
app, or an email checkout, so they need about 5 minutes of your hands.

They're ordered by how much you get for the effort.

---

## 1. KitBash3D Cargo — 480+ assets ⭐ biggest win

The single highest-value item on this list. Cargo 3.0 (April 2026) gives free
accounts **480+ models, materials, textures and HDRIs** from KitBash3D *and*
Greyscalegorilla, with one-click import directly into Blender.

1. Download Cargo: https://kitbash3d.com/pages/cargo
2. Create a free account (no card required)
3. Install the Blender plugin from inside Cargo
4. Filter the library to **Free** and download what you want

These are the same production-grade assets as their paid kits — the free tier is
a curated subset, not a lower-quality tier.

**Also grab the standalone free kits** — separate from Cargo:
- Free collection: https://kitbash3d.com/collections/free-3d-assets
- **Neo City mini-kit**: https://kitbash3d.com/products/mini-kit-neo-city
  (pulls buildings from their paid Neo Tokyo, Cyber Streets, and Neo Shanghai
  worlds — excellent cyberpunk city geometry)

⚠️ **License:** Renders are fine. Redistribution is not — don't ship these
inside a game build or resell them as an asset pack. Check their terms before
client work.

→ Suggested destination: `03_Architecture_Structures/`

## 2. Quixel Megascans via Fab — the entire library, free

Epic made the **whole** Megascans library free with an Epic Games account.
Photoscanned rock, debris, architecture, and surfaces — the fastest way to make
kitbashed scenes stop looking like grey clay.

1. Sign in at https://www.fab.com with a free Epic account
2. Filter to Megascans, add to your library
3. Export to Blender via the Quixel Bridge app, or download and import manually

→ Suggested destination: `01_Nature_and_Organic/` and `02_Industrial_and_Machinery/`

## 3. BlenderKit — built into Blender already

Free tier, browsable from inside Blender's Asset Browser — no separate download.

1. In Blender: `Edit ▸ Preferences ▸ Add-ons`, search "BlenderKit", enable it
2. Create a free account when prompted
3. Browse from the BlenderKit panel; free assets drag straight into the scene

Good starting points:
- Urban environments: https://www.blenderkit.com/?query=category_subtree%3Acityspace
- Buildings: https://www.blenderkit.com/?query=category_subtree%3Abuilding
- Modular City Blocks (6 buildings + 5 street modules):
  https://www.blenderkit.com/asset-gallery-detail/9a0c1d77-864d-4b05-b553-6580b1b107ad/

## On pirated copies of paid kits

You'd asked about leaked copies of paid packs — I left those out deliberately,
and it's worth knowing why beyond the legal side.

Blender `.blend` files can carry **embedded Python that executes on load**. Warez
hosts are a known malware vector, and a tampered `.blend` is a genuine
code-execution risk on your Mac, not a theoretical one. There's no safe way to
vet one short of opening it in a sandbox with auto-run scripts disabled.

The practical answer: the free tier is unusually strong right now. Cargo's 480
assets, the full Megascans library, and the 521 CC0 Poly Haven models already in
this folder cover most of what the paid kits offer. KitBash3D also runs periodic
free-kit giveaways — worth watching their free collection page.
